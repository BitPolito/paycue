How it works

One event, one payout, never two

01 · TRIGGEREvent

A coin is hit, a pull request is merged. Signed and checked before anything else.

02 · POLICYRules

Allow, deny with a reason, or hold. Budgets count payments still in flight.

03 · ROUTEDestination

A Lightning Address, or L-USDT on Liquid through KaleidoSwap.

04 · PROVIDERPayment

The node pays the invoice. Uncertain is never treated as failed.

05 · EVIDENCEAudit

Every decision is recorded: who decided what, and when.
